About Abdul

A master tiler since sixteen

Abdul started tiling at 16 as an apprentice. Before long he was doing better work than the boss who trained him, so he set up as his own master tiler.

Since then he has tiled and fitted bathrooms in Syria, in Lebanon, and now in Scotland. The standard has stayed the same everywhere: straight lines, tight joints, and a finish he'd be happy to have in his own home.

Customers know him as a good man to have in the house. He turns up when he says he will, keeps the work tidy, and doesn't leave until the job is right.

How we work

A new bathroom in about three days

Most full bathroom refits take three working days. You can still use your bathroom while we're working. Just let us know and give us a moment to set it up for you.

  1. Before

    Free visit & quote

    We come out, measure up, talk through what you want and give you a clear price.

  2. Day 1

    Strip-out & prep

    The old bathroom comes out and the room is prepared for the new one.

  3. Day 2

    Plumbing, framing & tiling

    Waste pipes and plumbing go in, frames go up and tiling begins.

  4. Day 3

    Finished bathroom

    Tiling is completed, everything is fitted and the room is cleaned and handed back to you.
